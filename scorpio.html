Scorpio

October 23 – November 21

Scorpio is the eighth sign of the zodiac. It is associated with determination, emotional depth, transformation, loyalty, and uncovering the truth beneath the surface.

Scorpio Quick Facts

Element
Water
Modality
Fixed
Ruling Planets
Pluto & Mars
Symbol
The Scorpion

Strengths

Scorpio is often described as determined, loyal, resourceful, intuitive, passionate, and emotionally resilient. They are known for their ability to stay focused on long-term goals.

Challenges

Scorpio may sometimes become secretive, overly guarded, suspicious, or have difficulty letting go after experiencing emotional hurt or betrayal.

Relationships

In relationships, Scorpio often values trust, honesty, commitment, emotional depth, loyalty, and forming meaningful, lasting connections.

Scorpio in Everyday Life

Scorpio energy is associated with transformation, determination, and understanding what lies beneath the surface. People with strong Scorpio placements often approach life with focus, emotional insight, and a desire to grow through life's experiences.

Scorpio is a fixed sign, which means it is connected with persistence, dedication, and staying committed to important goals. As a water sign, Scorpio is also associated with emotion, intuition, empathy, and personal transformation.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Scorpio traits are expressed.

Scorpio in a Birth Chart

Scorpio may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have a Scorpio Moon, Scorpio rising sign, or several planets placed in Scorpio even if Scorpio is not their Sun sign.
