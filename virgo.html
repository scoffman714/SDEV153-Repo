Virgo

August 23 – September 22

Virgo is the sixth sign of the zodiac. It is associated with organization, careful observation, practical thinking, service, and the desire to improve everyday life.

Virgo Quick Facts

Element
Earth
Modality
Mutable
Ruling Planet
Mercury
Symbol
The Maiden

Strengths

Virgo is often described as practical, observant, dependable, thoughtful, organized, helpful, and skilled at noticing important details.

Challenges

Virgo may sometimes become overly critical, worry about small details, expect perfection, or have difficulty recognizing their own progress.

Relationships

In relationships, Virgo often values reliability, thoughtful effort, clear communication, practical support, trust, and shared responsibility.

Virgo in Everyday Life

Virgo energy is associated with solving problems, creating useful systems, and improving everyday routines. People with strong Virgo placements may enjoy helping others and making complicated situations more manageable.

Virgo is a mutable sign, which means it is connected with adaptability, adjustment, and responding to changing needs. As an earth sign, Virgo is also associated with practicality, responsibility, organization, and careful planning.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Virgo traits are expressed.

Virgo in a Birth Chart

Virgo may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have a Virgo Moon, Virgo rising sign, or several planets placed in Virgo even if Virgo is not their Sun sign.
