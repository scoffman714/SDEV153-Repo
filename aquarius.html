Aquarius

January 20 – February 18

Aquarius is the eleventh sign of the zodiac. It is associated with originality, independence, innovation, community, and imagining new possibilities for the future.

Aquarius Quick Facts

Element
Air
Modality
Fixed
Ruling Planets
Uranus & Saturn
Symbol
The Water Bearer

Strengths

Aquarius is often described as inventive, independent, open-minded, thoughtful, idealistic, friendly, and willing to question traditional ideas.

Challenges

Aquarius may sometimes appear emotionally distant, become stubborn about their ideas, resist expectations, or focus on logic while overlooking emotional concerns.

Relationships

In relationships, Aquarius often values friendship, honesty, independence, intellectual connection, acceptance, and the freedom to be authentic.

Aquarius in Everyday Life

Aquarius energy is associated with innovation, community, and imagining new possibilities. People with strong Aquarius placements may be drawn to unusual ideas, social causes, technology, or collaborative change.

Aquarius is a fixed sign, which means it is connected with persistence, commitment, and maintaining strongly held ideals. As an air sign, Aquarius is also associated with thought, communication, observation, and collective awareness.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Aquarius traits are expressed.

Aquarius in a Birth Chart

Aquarius may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have an Aquarius Moon, Aquarius rising sign, or several planets placed in Aquarius even if Aquarius is not their Sun sign.
