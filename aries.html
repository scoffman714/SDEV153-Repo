Aries

March 21 – April 19

Aries is the first sign of the zodiac. It is associated with energy, courage, independence, action, and new beginnings.

Aries Quick Facts

Element
Fire
Modality
Cardinal
Ruling Planet
Mars
Symbol
The Ram

Strengths

Aries is often described as confident, enthusiastic, direct, motivated, brave, and willing to take initiative.

Challenges

Aries may sometimes act impatiently, become frustrated quickly, or rush into situations before considering every detail.

Relationships

In relationships, Aries often values honesty, excitement, independence, loyalty, and open communication.

Aries in Everyday Life

Aries energy is associated with starting projects, taking risks, and pursuing goals with enthusiasm. People with strong Aries placements may enjoy challenges and situations that allow them to act independently.

Aries is a cardinal sign, which means it is connected with initiative and leadership. As a fire sign, Aries is also associated with passion, confidence, creativity, and action.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Aries traits are expressed.

Aries in a Birth Chart

Aries may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have an Aries Moon, Aries rising sign, or several planets placed in Aries even if Aries is not their Sun sign.
