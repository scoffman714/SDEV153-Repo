Pisces

February 19 – March 20

Pisces is the twelfth and final sign of the zodiac. It is associated with compassion, imagination, intuition, creativity, and empathy for others.

Pisces Quick Facts

Element
Water
Modality
Mutable
Ruling Planets
Neptune & Jupiter
Symbol
The Fish

Strengths

Pisces is often described as compassionate, imaginative, intuitive, gentle, creative, understanding, and deeply empathetic toward others.

Challenges

Pisces may sometimes become overly idealistic, avoid difficult situations, absorb the emotions of others, or struggle with setting healthy boundaries.

Relationships

In relationships, Pisces often values kindness, emotional connection, trust, compassion, understanding, and supporting the people they care about.

Pisces in Everyday Life

Pisces energy is associated with imagination, creativity, and seeing the world through compassion and intuition. People with strong Pisces placements often enjoy artistic expression, helping others, and exploring their inner world.

Pisces is a mutable sign, which means it is connected with adaptability, flexibility, and flowing with change. As a water sign, Pisces is also associated with emotion, empathy, intuition, and imagination.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Pisces traits are expressed.

Pisces in a Birth Chart

Pisces may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have a Pisces Moon, Pisces rising sign, or several planets placed in Pisces even if Pisces is not their Sun sign.
