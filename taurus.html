Taurus

April 20 – May 20

Taurus is the second sign of the zodiac. It is associated with stability, patience, reliability, comfort, and an appreciation for life's simple pleasures.

Taurus Quick Facts

Element
Earth
Modality
Fixed
Ruling Planet
Venus
Symbol
The Bull

Strengths

Taurus is often described as dependable, patient, loyal, practical, calm, and determined. They usually prefer steady progress and value long-lasting relationships.

Challenges

Taurus may sometimes resist change, become stubborn, or stay in familiar situations longer than necessary because they appreciate stability and routine.

Relationships

In relationships, Taurus often values loyalty, trust, consistency, affection, and building a strong sense of security with the people they love.

Taurus in Everyday Life

Taurus energy is associated with patience, persistence, and creating a comfortable life. People with strong Taurus placements often enjoy working toward long-term goals one step at a time.

Taurus is a fixed sign, which means it is connected with consistency, determination, and maintaining stability. As an earth sign, Taurus is also associated with practicality, reliability, and the physical world.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Taurus traits are expressed.

Taurus in a Birth Chart

Taurus may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have a Taurus Moon, Taurus rising sign, or several planets placed in Taurus even if Taurus is not their Sun sign.
