Cancer

June 21 – July 22

Cancer is the fourth sign of the zodiac. It is associated with compassion, intuition, emotional depth, family, and creating a sense of comfort and belonging.

Cancer Quick Facts

Element
Water
Modality
Cardinal
Ruling Planet
The Moon
Symbol
The Crab

Strengths

Cancer is often described as caring, loyal, intuitive, nurturing, compassionate, and deeply connected to the people they love.

Challenges

Cancer may sometimes become overly sensitive, hold onto past hurts, withdraw emotionally, or have difficulty letting go of emotional experiences.

Relationships

In relationships, Cancer often values trust, emotional security, loyalty, kindness, and building a strong sense of home with the people closest to them.

Cancer in Everyday Life

Cancer energy is associated with caring for others, protecting loved ones, and creating spaces where people feel safe and supported. People with strong Cancer placements often value close relationships and emotional connection.

Cancer is a cardinal sign, which means it is connected with taking initiative and creating new beginnings. As a water sign, Cancer is also associated with emotions, intuition, empathy, and imagination.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Cancer traits are expressed.

Cancer in a Birth Chart

Cancer may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have a Cancer Moon, Cancer rising sign, or several planets placed in Cancer even if Cancer is not their Sun sign.
