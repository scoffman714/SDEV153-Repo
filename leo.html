Leo

July 23 – August 22

Leo is the fifth sign of the zodiac. It is associated with confidence, creativity, generosity, leadership, and expressing yourself with warmth and enthusiasm.

Leo Quick Facts

Element
Fire
Modality
Fixed
Ruling Planet
The Sun
Symbol
The Lion

Strengths

Leo is often described as confident, generous, creative, loyal, enthusiastic, and naturally encouraging of the people around them.

Challenges

Leo may sometimes become stubborn, seek too much recognition, or take criticism personally because they care deeply about expressing themselves.

Relationships

In relationships, Leo often values loyalty, affection, honesty, appreciation, and spending meaningful time with the people they care about.

Leo in Everyday Life

Leo energy is associated with creativity, confidence, and sharing talents with others. People with strong Leo placements often enjoy inspiring those around them and expressing themselves through hobbies, leadership, or artistic pursuits.

Leo is a fixed sign, which means it is connected with determination, consistency, and staying committed to personal goals. As a fire sign, Leo is also associated with passion, courage, optimism, and self-expression.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Leo traits are expressed.

Leo in a Birth Chart

Leo may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have a Leo Moon, Leo rising sign, or several planets placed in Leo even if Leo is not their Sun sign.
