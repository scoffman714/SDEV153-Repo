Understanding Your Birth Chart

A birth chart shows the positions of the Sun, Moon, planets, zodiac signs, and houses at a particular moment.

The Main Parts of a Birth Chart

  • Planets: Represent different drives and needs.
  • Signs: Describe how planetary energy is expressed.
  • Houses: Represent different areas of life.
  • Aspects: Show relationships between planets.

Planets in a Birth Chart

In astrology, the planets represent different parts of your personality, motivations, emotions, and life experiences. The zodiac sign connected to each planet describes how that planet's energy may be expressed.

The Sun

The Sun is associated with identity, purpose, confidence, and self-expression. It represents the qualities a person may be learning to develop throughout life.

The Moon

The Moon is associated with emotions, instincts, habits, comfort, and inner needs. It can describe how a person responds emotionally.

The Rising Sign

The rising sign is associated with first impressions, appearance, and how a person approaches new situations. It is determined by the sign rising on the eastern horizon at the time of birth.

Other planets, including Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn, represent additional parts of a person's communication style, relationships, motivation, growth, and responsibilities.

The Twelve Houses

A birth chart is divided into twelve sections called houses. Each house represents a different area of life where planetary energy may appear.

Beginner Guide to the Twelve Houses
House Area of Life
First House Identity, appearance, first impressions, and approach to life
Second House Money, possessions, personal values, and security
Third House Communication, learning, siblings, and local surroundings
Fourth House Home, family, roots, and private life
Fifth House Creativity, romance, hobbies, pleasure, and self-expression
Sixth House Daily routines, work, service, habits, and wellness
Seventh House Partnerships, close relationships, and commitments
Eighth House Shared resources, transformation, intimacy, and change
Ninth House Travel, higher education, beliefs, and personal growth
Tenth House Career, reputation, achievements, and public life
Eleventh House Friendships, groups, community, and future goals
Twelfth House Reflection, privacy, spirituality, and the subconscious

Understanding Planetary Aspects

Planetary aspects describe the angles that planets form with one another in a birth chart. These connections show how the energies of the planets interact, either harmoniously or with some tension.

Conjunction

A conjunction occurs when two planets are positioned very close together. Their energies combine and become more powerful. Depending on the planets involved, this combination can feel supportive, intense, or challenging.

Opposition

An opposition occurs when two planets are located directly across from one another in the birth chart. It can create tension between two different needs or areas of life, encouraging balance and compromise.

Trine

A trine is a harmonious aspect that allows the energies of two planets to work together naturally. It often represents talents, strengths, or areas of life that feel comfortable and easy.

Square

A square is a challenging aspect that may create conflict, frustration, or obstacles. Although squares can feel difficult, they can also encourage personal growth, action, and change.

Sextile

A sextile is a supportive aspect that represents opportunities, cooperation, and potential. Its positive energy may require some effort or initiative before its benefits are fully experienced.
