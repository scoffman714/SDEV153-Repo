Capricorn

December 22 – January 19

Capricorn is the tenth sign of the zodiac. It is associated with responsibility, ambition, discipline, patience, and achieving long-term goals through steady effort.

Capricorn Quick Facts

Element
Earth
Modality
Cardinal
Ruling Planet
Saturn
Symbol
The Sea-Goat

Strengths

Capricorn is often described as responsible, disciplined, dependable, patient, practical, hardworking, and determined to accomplish meaningful goals.

Challenges

Capricorn may sometimes become overly serious, place too much pressure on themselves, resist asking for help, or focus so heavily on responsibilities that they forget to relax.

Relationships

In relationships, Capricorn often values loyalty, stability, reliability, mutual respect, and building a strong future together over time.

Capricorn in Everyday Life

Capricorn energy is associated with planning ahead, working toward long-term success, and taking responsibility seriously. People with strong Capricorn placements often enjoy setting goals and seeing the results of their dedication over time.

Capricorn is a cardinal sign, which means it is connected with taking initiative and building lasting foundations. As an earth sign, Capricorn is also associated with practicality, organization, perseverance, and steady progress.

A Sun sign is only one part of a complete birth chart. A person's Moon, rising sign, planets, houses, and aspects can all influence how Capricorn traits are expressed.

Capricorn in a Birth Chart

Capricorn may appear in more than one area of a birth chart. A person could have a Capricorn Moon, Capricorn rising sign, or several planets placed in Capricorn even if Capricorn is not their Sun sign.
